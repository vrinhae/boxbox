How to read this

A Race order

The timing screen at the end of the selected lap. LEC is the ego driver: the only car the agents control. Every other car replays its real race.

From lap_state events

B Pit wall

What happened inside the system on this lap: what triggered the Race Engineer, which agents it called, what they said, any send-back rounds, the plan and the final call. Most laps have no trigger, so no LLM calls are made. The counter shows the running total.

From route, agent_msg, plan, decision

C Strategy timeline

Each bar is a stint, coloured by tyre. The top two rows put the agents' strategy for LEC next to what LEC really did, so you can see where they split.

From lap_state + decision

D Gaps

Did the undercut work? Watch the gap to the car ahead after a stop. Switch to Real strategy at the top to compare the two runs.
